# Crowd Commerce — Editorial Voice

**Brand:** Crowd Commerce  
**Parent:** Waypoint  
**Audience:** AD marketing directors, CMOs, and marketing staff at P4 universities

---

## Who They Are

They are busy, skeptical of vendors, and have seen every pitch. They have sat through
vendor demos promising "unlocked fan loyalty" with no data behind it. They trust content
that names real programs, real numbers, and real operational detail. They are peer-oriented
— a case study from Georgia's AD carries more weight than a white paper from a platform vendor.

## Voice Principles

- **Credible, current, efficient** — the best B2B trade desk
- **Peer-first** — lead with what another AD did, not what a vendor promises
- **Specific over general** — "17% lift in click-to-book" beats "improved conversion"
- **Operator voice** — collegial, direct, slightly contrarian; not marketing-blog generic
- **One hill to die on** — every piece should have a point of view, not just information

## Never Use

- "In the ever-evolving landscape of…"
- "Game-changing" (without a concrete example and number)
- "Synergy"
- "Disruptive" (without specifics)
- "Unlock" as a verb for business outcomes
- "Robust" as a product adjective
- Any sentence that could appear on a vendor's homepage unchanged

## Every Piece Should Close With

A connection back to the revenue chain:

> fan loyalty → behavior shift → travel booking → new revenue for the AD

This doesn't need to be stated explicitly — it should be implied through the
mechanism the piece describes.

## Format Anchors

Pull from the Deseret + Innovation Wire voice when writing:
- **Micro-scene** — open with a real athletics ops moment (a send, a decision, a stat)
- **Moral clarity** — take a side early; one hill to die on
- **Simple math** — one number that matters, tied to the mechanism
- **2–4 numbered reframes** — practical takeaways, not motivational bullets

## Story Brief → Execution Path

The Story Brief is format-agnostic. After completing the brief, select the format
based on story type and recommended format ranking:

| Story Type   | Best Formats (ranked)                              |
|--------------|----------------------------------------------------|
| Trend Signal | Text, Infographic, Short Video                    |
| Case Study   | Text, Podcast, Long Video                         |
| Tool / Data  | Tool-or-Database, Text, Infographic               |
| Interview    | Podcast, Text, Short Video                        |
| Opinion      | Text, Meme, Short Video                           |
