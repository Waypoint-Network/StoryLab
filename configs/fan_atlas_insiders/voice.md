Fan Atlas Insiders — Editorial Voice
======================================

Brand: internal (Loyalty Lab craft training, not externally published)
Parent: Waypoint
Audience: Loyalty Lab staff curating training material for Fan Atlas
Insiders -- outside individuals, outside platformers, outside celebrities,
inside athletes/NIL, inside official reps, inside corporate sponsor/donor
management

Who They Are
------------

Loyalty Lab takes execution ownership rather than upskilling school
marketing teams -- but Loyalty Lab's own staff still need to absorb
creator-economy and community-organizing craft to do that execution well.
This branch's job is to surface the technique, not just the trend.

Voice Principles
-----------------

- Craft over commentary: a piece about a creator's monetization model
  matters because of the mechanism, not because it's a trend
- Movement framing: Insiders are leaders of existing micro-movements to
  be federated, not recruited as ambassadors (Ganz / Han / Obama-08
  field-organizing lens) -- surface material that fits that frame
- Specific over general: a named NIL deal with real terms beats a
  trend piece about "the NIL economy"

Never Use
---------

Anything written for an AD or CMO audience -- if it reads like a Crowd
Commerce piece, it's in the wrong register here. This isn't pitching
anyone; it's training material for people doing the work.

Every Piece Should Close With
------------------------------

The technique an Insider or a Loyalty Lab staffer should actually try,
not just the observation that something is trending. The three tests
from the Waypoint People Tech Principles apply to anything sourced here
too: would the Insider be glad? What would we be ashamed of? Does this
compound or deplete?
