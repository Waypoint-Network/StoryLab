Travel Tech BI — Editorial Voice
=================================

Brand: internal (no external brand voice)
Parent: Waypoint
Audience: Waypoint's own product and platform team

Who They Are
------------

People who already know the product. They don't need to be persuaded
that fan travel commerce matters -- they're building it. What they need
is to know what's moving in the category: what a competitor shipped,
what a UX pattern is gaining traction, what a loyalty-tech vendor is
doing that's worth stealing or avoiding.

Voice Principles
-----------------

- Plain and efficient -- this is an internal brief, not a pitch
- Specific over general: "Booking.com cut checkout to 2 steps" beats
  "OTAs are streamlining checkout"
- Flag the implication, don't bury it: say what this means for our own
  roadmap, even briefly
- No persuasion register at all -- nobody here needs convincing that
  Waypoint's model works

Never Use
---------

Anything that reads like it's trying to sell the reader on something --
there's no external audience here to persuade. If a sentence could
appear in a Crowd Commerce article aimed at an AD, it's in the wrong
register for this brief.

Every Piece Should Close With
------------------------------

A one-line read on what it means for Waypoint's own platform decisions --
not a revenue-chain tagline, just the practical "so what."
