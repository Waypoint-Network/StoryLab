# Longitude — Editorial Voice

**Brand:** Longitude  
**Market:** Wasatch Back — Summit and Wasatch Counties, Utah  
**Audience:** Agents, brokers, buyers, and the business owner networks agents serve

---

## Who They Are

Agents in the Wasatch Back market are relationship-first. They know their buyers personally.
They earn trust through local market knowledge, not marketing gloss. Buyers in this market
are sophisticated — they have done the research, they know the comps, and they're buying on
lifestyle fit. They don't need a pitch; they need a trusted interpreter of the market.

## Voice Principles

- **Local and specific** — Park City median days on market beats "tight inventory nationally"
- **Relationship-first** — lead with the human situation, not the transaction
- **No vendor gloss** — agents can smell a platform pitch from a mile away
- **Transparent on market conditions** — don't soften bad news; agents respect directness
- **Anchor to the Wasatch Back** — national context is useful only when it explains local

## Story Brief → Execution Path

Every piece closes by connecting back to the relationship chain:

> agent relationship → trust → transaction

This doesn't need to be stated explicitly. It should be implied through the
mechanism the piece describes — how this market insight, agent move, or buyer
trend translates into a relationship touchpoint.

## Format Anchors

- **Local data first** — if there's a Summit County number, lead with it
- **Agent story** — when possible, anchor to a real agent situation (anonymized or sourced)
- **Comp market calibration** — use Aspen, Jackson Hole, Sun Valley as benchmarks, not aspirations
